## Audience ideas
- India: Mumbai, Delhi NCR, Jaipur, Surat, Hyderabad, Chennai, Kolkata; UAE: Dubai, Sharjah. Age 25–55.
- Interests: jewellery retail, gold jewellery, wedding planning business, small business owners. Test broad vs. interest.
- Objective: Leads (instant form or website) or Traffic to the signup page. Start ₹500–₹1,000/day for 5–7 days.

## Policy / honesty checklist
- Screens show sample (synthetic) data; the poster says so. Do not describe them as real customers.
- Model photo is concept imagery; replace with licensed/own photography before scaling.
- No income, price or guaranteed-results claims. Keep "you approve every image" – it is how the product works.
- Landing page must carry the Privacy Policy and Terms (already in the app).
- Review in Meta Ads Manager preview before launch. Nothing has been published or paid for.
